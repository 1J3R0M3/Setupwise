Privacy

Setupwise has no telemetry, no analytics, no account and no servers of its own.

It connects to the internet only for:

  * Installing, updating and searching apps - through winget (part of
    Windows), only when you ask for it.

  * App icons - Setupwise loads the homepage and icon of each app from the
    vendor's own website. This transfers your IP address to that website.
    Turn off: Settings > Privacy > Load app icons

  * Update check - at startup Setupwise asks GitHub (api.github.com) whether
    a newer version exists. This transfers your IP address to GitHub.
    Turn off: Settings > Privacy > Check for Setupwise updates

Settings, logs and cached icons stay on your PC
(%APPDATA%\Setupwise and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Setupwise).

Full privacy policy:
